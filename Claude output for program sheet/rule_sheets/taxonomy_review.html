The same manual label gets two different CAPP answers — on the same part

Vertical Planar Face is the label behind every single clean 1:1 match on Top Plate (14 of 14), and half of Bottom Plate's (6 of 8). CAPP calls some of those clusters planar_face and others pocket — for faces the labeler described identically.

This isn't a taxonomy-wording question like the others below. It's the rule‑based classifier (3. classify_features.py) drawing an inconsistent line between a flat wall and a pocket floor. Worth root‑causing before locking in a mapping for this label at all.
